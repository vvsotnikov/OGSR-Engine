# Legacy group reconciliation

PR #3 now includes merged main through `ea325cc57`, including the DirectInput check and HRESULT message-lifetime fixes. This increment separates the legacy group's live-member scan policy (`stops_group_offline_scan`) from dead-member detach/registry maintenance (`detach_dead_group_member`). Both are file-local helpers; no fields, save format or virtual methods change.

Cleanup remains in its original traversal position. The preserved behavior is:

- Non-monsters and living members that cannot switch offline are skipped.
- A living member that cannot switch online, or is within the offline threshold (or uses whole-map mode), stops the scan. Later dead members remain untouched.
- Dead members encountered before a stop are erased, temporarily marked offline, detached if appropriate, registered separately and removed from graph-point membership only if still unattached. Attachment is queried again after registration. The member returns online and the group count decreases.
- An empty group returns. Otherwise the group must permit going offline, and then switches if it permits going online **or** the scan reached its end. This unusual final condition is unchanged, even when a live member stopped the scan.

A full cleanup pass followed by a separate eligibility pass would change those semantics. This extraction does not enable skipping recurring group work or claim a performance improvement. Event-only switching remains disabled.
